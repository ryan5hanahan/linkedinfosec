After twenty-five years in technology, most of it making security and IT work for organizations that couldn't justify a full team, I've set up Linked Information Security as the home for that work.

The short version of what I do: fractional security leadership for lean organizations. Audit readiness for SOC 2 and HIPAA. Incident readiness, and guiding the response when something happens. Getting an application ready for a proper penetration test. Keeping a small AWS footprint secure. The [services section](/#services) has the details.

What I plan to write here is the kind of thing I find myself explaining on calls. How to get through a first SOC 2 audit without drowning in it. What a lean company actually needs from incident response. Why persuasive design belongs in a threat model. Short pieces, written to be useful, published when I have something worth saying rather than on a schedule.

Posts also go to [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryan-shanahan), and there's an [RSS feed](/blog/feed.xml) if you prefer that.
