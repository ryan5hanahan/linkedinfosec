Services

Security work you can act on.

For startups, healthcare and professional services firms, and regional teams that need experienced help turning security decisions into finished work. Every engagement is scoped up front and delivered by me, not handed off.

  1. Fractional CISO

    A security roadmap, a clear picture of your risks, policies that fit your size, and a senior voice for your board, customers, and auditors.

    Served as CISO reporting to the CEO at a digital health company, owning security, IT, and vendor risk.

  2. SOC 2 and HIPAA readiness

    Control mapping, evidence collection, auditor coordination, and remediation. You leave with owners, dates, and an audit you can pass.

    Built a first formal audit program and led three SOC 2 Type II cycles with HIPAA in scope, using NIST CSF and CIS Controls.

  3. Incident readiness and response guidance

    Plans, roles, and tabletop exercises before anything happens. When something does, I guide your response, coordinate with your providers, and help you retain a dedicated incident response firm if the situation calls for one.

    Wrote and exercised incident response, continuity, and recovery plans with quarterly drills and tabletops. Led a client ransomware response from containment through recovery.

  4. Penetration test readiness

    An internal review of your web applications and APIs before you engage a dedicated testing firm, so their time goes to depth rather than the obvious. I help scope the engagement, select the firm, and turn findings into work your developers can act on.

    GWAPT certified. SANS SEC542 teaching assistant. Performed internal web application tests, added Semgrep and Trivy to CI pipelines, and coordinated independent external assessments.

  5. Cloud security and AWS operations

    Hardening, monitoring, backups, and patching for small AWS estates, with cost reviewed alongside risk rather than after it.

    Migrated databases to RDS, added WAF and load balancing, deployed a SIEM, and formalized monitoring and patching across production, staging, and development environments.
